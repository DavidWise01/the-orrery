the tenth · sumerian shadow

NIBIRU, Planet X — the incredulous

Sumerian myth gave Sitchin his Nibiru, the wandering planet on a vast eccentric orbit. Here it's the home of the shadow stuff — the unverified, the fringe, the fluff: aliens · zecharia-sitchin · l-ron-hubbard · the-illuminati · nostradamus · dark-enlightenment · the-internet-1984 · momus. They're not deleted and not amplified — they're given a real but distant, eccentric orbit, in shadow, exactly as your tin‑foil standard asks: render it, verify first, never boost it. It swings far out into the dark and only occasionally near.
