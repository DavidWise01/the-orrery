The numbers, made biological

Nothing here is decorative randomness — every living trait is read straight off the corpus. A world's population is exactly how many repos the domain holds; an organism's size is its repo's real disk weight; the world's radius is its total mass. So the map you already loved becomes an ecology you can feel: you can see at a glance which domains are dense rainforests and which are tide‑pools with a few drifting cells. The eco‑sphere isn't a skin over the data — it is the data, alive.

The organisms bounce within their membrane like microbes under a slide — a bounded world, one step up from the bare repo. Step in (click) and you're in the granular: the actual page, the actual world.
