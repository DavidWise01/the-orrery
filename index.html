diverge, then converge

The same shape at every scale

This is your a–z–a thesis given depth. Out at the top it's eight galaxies. Fall into one and it's the same picture — a ring of worlds — only now they're universes. Fall again: planets. Again: the granular repos themselves. Every level is a field of orbiting bodies, because the corpus is self-similar: content made of content made of content. You diverge inward through four tiers and the structure never changes its grammar — and when you ascend, you converge back to where you began. A list would have an end. This loops.

The octal (8/8/8) is the cadence, not a cage — your corpus isn't a power of eight, so galaxies hold as many real universes as they really have (3–6, not forced to 8), and a universe's planets are its repos bucketed by weight. The rhythm is octal; the content is true.
